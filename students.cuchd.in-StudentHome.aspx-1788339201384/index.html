“POST MATRIC SCHOLARSHIP CHANDIGARH for SC/OBC/EBC STUDENTS”

This is to inform to all the eligible SC/ST/OBC/EBC students belongs to CHANDIGARH (UT) for “POST-MATRIC SCHOLARSHIP SCHEME FOR CHANDIGARH (UT) SC/OBC/EBC STUDENTS” for the academic session 2026-2027 for Fresh & Renewal.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:

  • For SC Students – The annual family income of the parent/guardians from all sources in case of SC/ST students should not exceed Rs. 2,50,000/- per annum.
  • FOR OBC Students - The annual family income of the parent/guardians from all sources in case of OBC students should not exceed Rs. 2,50,000/- per annum.
  • FOR EBC Students - The annual family income of the parent/guardians from all sources in case of EBC students should not exceed Rs. 2,50,000/- per annum.

  • All SC/OBC/EBC Students pursuing their studies in Chandigarh University - (Students are advised to visit website www.http://scholarships.gov.in/ State Schemes/Chandigarh.

  • Student's recent passport-size 1 photograph.
  • Photocopy of Residence Certificate (Issued by Chandigarh Administration).
  • For Fresh Applications: Self-attested copy of the Class 12 (Previous Academic Year) marksheet.
  • For Renewal Applications: Self-attested copy of the previous year's marksheet/result.
  • Scan copy of Proof of bank account in the student's name and Photocopy of Aadhaar Card.
  • Photocopy of Category Certificate (Issued by an officer not below the rank of Tehsildar).
  • Photocopy of the Income Certificate for the Financial Year 2026–27 (Issued by an officer not below the rank of Tehsildar).
  • Original Fee Structure (current academic session).
  • Original Gap Year Affidavit, for Post Matric Scholarship Scheme for SC/ST/OBC/EBC Students for Fresh student.

CIRCULAR: POST-MATRIC SCHOLARSHIP SCHEME FOR SCHEDULED CASTE (SC) STUDENTS OF PUNJAB STATE.

  • 05 Aug 2026
  • 11:20:03

CIRCULAR

Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme for Scheduled Caste (SC) Students of Punjab State.

Academic Session 2026–27

This is for the information of all eligible Scheduled Caste (SC) students belonging to Punjab State that applications are invited under the Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme for Scheduled Caste Students (Punjab State) through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) for the Academic Session 2026–27.

The scholarship is applicable to both Fresh and Renewal students.

1. Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must fulfill the following conditions:

  • Must belong to the Scheduled Caste (SC) category of Punjab State.
  • Annual family income from all sources should not exceed ?2,50,000/-.
  • Must possess a valid Aadhaar number linked with the student's bank account.
  • Must satisfy all other eligibility conditions prescribed by the Government of Punjab.

2. Online Application Process

All eligible students are required to apply online through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) by following the steps below:

  • Register on NSP using Aadhaar-based registration and complete One-Time Registration (OTR), wherever applicable.
  • Log in to the NSP Portal and select Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme for Scheduled Caste Students (Punjab State).
  • Fill in all personal, academic, and bank account details carefully.
  • Upload the following mandatory documents in PDF/JPG/JPEG format (file size: 20 KB to 500 KB):
    • Previous academic year examination marksheet.
    • Scheduled Caste Certificate issued by the competent authority.
    • Family Income Certificate issued by the competent authority (issued on or after 01 April 2026).
    • Freeship Card issued by Govt of Punjab
  • Verify all details carefully before final submission.
  • Download and keep a copy of the submitted application for future reference.

3. Submission of Hard Copy for Institute Verification

After successful online submission on NSP, students must submit one complete set of the following documents to their respective Department SC Coordinator/Scholarship Branch for Institute and Govt. Verification as per check list attached:

  • Printed and signed NSP Application Form with photograph pasted (do not staple).
  • Signed Freeship Card application form.
  • Three Passport Size Photographs.
  • Income Certificate of parent (issued on or after 01 April 2026 by an officer not below the rank of Tehsildar).
  • Self-attested copy of Scheduled Caste Certificate (issued by an officer not below the rank of Tehsildar and not older than five years).
  • Self-attested copy of 10th DMC.
  • Self-attested copy of previous year's DMC/Result.
  • Original Gap Year Certificate (if applicable).
  • Self-attested copy of Aadhaar-seeded bank passbook.
  • Self-attested copy of Student Aadhaar Card.
  • Self-attested copy of Parent's Aadhaar Card.
  • Original Punjabi Undertaking.
  • Original Attendance Certificate (Annexure-I).
  • Self-attested copy of Student Identity Card.
  • Self-attested copy of Punjab Residence Certificate (issued by an officer not below the rank of Tehsildar and not older than five years).
  • Original Annexure-II duly signed by the Head of Department (Renewal students only).
  • Original Self-Declaration Form signed by the applicant.
  • Any other document, if required by the Government.

4. Important Instructions

  • Ensure that the student's name, parents' names, date of birth, Aadhaar details, and other personal information are identical in all documents.
  • The student's bank account must be active and Aadhaar seeded.
  • Incomplete, incorrect, or misleading applications are liable to be rejected.
  • Applications submitted after the prescribed last date will not be entertained.
  • Grant of scholarship is subject to verification by the Institute and approval by the Government of Punjab.

5. Last Date

The last date for submission of the online application through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) is: 15 October 2026

Students are advised to complete the online application and submit the required hard copy documents well before the closing date to avoid any last-minute inconvenience.

For any assistance regarding the scholarship application process, students may contact the Scholarship Branch between 10:30 AM and 3:30 PM.

  • Main Campus: Navjot Singh, Assistant Registrar, Scholarship Branch, Block B1, Room No. 204.
  • North Campus: Mr. Bhupinder Singh, Assistant Registrar, Registrar Office Room NO 206 Block E2.
  • South Campus: Mr. Narinder Singh, Office Executive, Block D4, Room No. 309.

Email: punjabpms@cumail.in
